  > HOW IT WORKS
  > 1. Sign up and connect your Google Drive (we only see folders we create)
  > 2. Create an event and download the QR code
  > 3. Guests scan the QR, enter their name, and upload photos directly from their browser
  > 4. All photos land in your Drive folder, organized and ready to download
  >

  > - Native iOS and Android apps for organizers
  > - Face ID / Touch ID unlock
  > - Share QR codes via the system share sheet (Messages, AirDrop, WhatsApp, email)

  > - Works offline-aware: clear messaging when your connection drops
  >
