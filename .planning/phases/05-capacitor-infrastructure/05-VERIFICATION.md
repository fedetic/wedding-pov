**Why human:** Requires physical Android device or emulator.

#### 5. iOS Google Drive OAuth via SFSafariViewController (CAP-05 — iOS)

**Test:** In the native iOS app, tap "Connect Google Drive". Watch what browser opens. Complete Google OAuth. Return to app.
